## Context

`packages/tzinfo/src/index.ts` `Timezone#periodForUtc` builds a period's start/end transitions from two sources: the tzdata isdst flips in `@blazetrails/date` (`tzdataIsdstFlips`, `packages/date/src/tzdata-isdst.ts`) and Temporal's `getTimeZoneTransition` (offset changes). A tzdata transition that changes only the abbreviation (no offset change, no isdst flip) is visible to neither, so the two periods around it share transitions and `TimezonePeriod#equals` calls them equal, where TZInfo 2's `TransitionsTimezonePeriod#==` says they differ (trails#9306, review item 5).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The vendored tzdata table (generated by `scripts/generate-tzdata-isdst.ts` from ruby tzinfo `transitions_up_to`) also records abbreviation-only transitions, or a sibling table does.
- [ ] `periodForUtc` uses them as period boundaries, and `TimezonePeriod#equals` tells the periods on either side apart.
- [ ] A test covers one real abbreviation-only transition, checked against ruby tzinfo.
