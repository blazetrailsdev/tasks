## Context

trails#9135 added `scripts/parity/eslint-count.ts` (`pnpm parity:lint:counts`, CI `parity:lint:counts:ci` in the Lint job). It counts ESLint violations per file and per rule for the changed files, against the PR merge commit's first parent. It replaced the exclude lists for `no-explicit-any`, `rails-error-parity` and `no-standalone-associations`.

Four ESLint exclude lists are still file- or site-membership allowlists:

- `eslint/expected-fixtures-exclude.json`, regenerated by `pnpm fixture-baseline:refresh` and checked by the CI step "ESLint exclude baselines up to date"
- `eslint/test-fixture-parity-exclude.json`, from `scripts/generate-fixture-parity-exclude.ts`
- `eslint/require-table-teardown-raw-sql-exclude.json`, read by `eslint.config.mjs` (`requireTableTeardownRawSqlExclude`)
- `eslint/no-ruby-compat-reimplementation-exclude.json`

## Acceptance criteria

- Each list's rule is added to `RULES` in `scripts/parity/eslint-count.ts` and held at `warn` in `eslint.config.mjs`. An arm that is not grandfathered today goes in `ZERO_BASELINE`.
- Each JSON list is deleted, along with its generator, its `eslint.config.mjs` wiring and its CI drift check.
- Where a rule's option, not the rule itself, carries the exclusion (`rawSql: false`), the counted arm is the one the option suppressed.
